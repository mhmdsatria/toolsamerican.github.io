Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01

Short version

Your files are processed in your own browser and are never uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on our servers. We do not sell personal data. Our privacy policy below explains the small amount of data that is collected to keep the site working and to serve ads.

1. Files you process with our tools

Every tool on Bytecore runs entirely client-side. Files you choose (PDFs, images, audio, and so on) are read by browser APIs and WebAssembly modules on your own device. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, never appear in logs, and never leave your browser. No copy is retained after the page is closed.

Because we never collect your files, we cannot scan, analyze, share, or recover them. You are solely responsible for backing up your own files.

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage trends, such as which pages and tools are popular and which browsers our visitors use. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect information such as your approximate location, browser type, device, and the pages you visit. This data is aggregated and is used to improve the site.

3. Advertising

We display advertisements through Google AdSense to keep the tools free. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. This may include personalized advertising.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google by visiting the Google Ads Settings. For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, see the Google privacy policy.

Advertisers may use third-party cookies to measure ad performance. We do not pass any file content or file data to advertisers.

4. Third-party data processors

  • Google Analytics — usage analytics. Data processed: technical usage data, cookies.
  • Google AdSense — advertising. Data processed: cookies related to ad delivery and measurement.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting. Data processed: standard web server logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) processed by the hosting provider.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Third parties (Google) may store cookies for analytics and advertising purposes as described above. We do not set first-party tracking cookies ourselves. You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings without impacting tool functionality, because the tools themselves work entirely in memory.

6. Data retention

We retain no files and no user-generated content on our servers. Analytics data is retained by Google per their policies. Logs from the hosting provider are subject to the provider's retention practices.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU under GDPR or in California under the CCPA), you may have rights including access, deletion, and opt-out of "sale" of personal information. Because we hold no personal information itself beyond what third-party processors collect, please contact the relevant processor (Google) directly, or email us and we will do our best to help.

8. Children's privacy

This site is a general audience service and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it where we are able.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The current version is always available at this page. Significant changes will be noted with a new effective date.

10. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to our contact page.
