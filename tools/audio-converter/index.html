Audio Converter

Convert audio files between formats for free — fast and private. This tool decodes your audio and re-encodes it as MP3, WAV, OGG, or M4A, using high-quality encoders that run entirely in your browser via WebAssembly. It is ideal for downsizing large WAV recordings to space-saving MP3, preparing ringtones, matching upload requirements, or archiving interviews. Because the processing happens locally on your device, sensitive recordings never travel to a server — the safest way to convert audio you do not want shared. No registration, no hidden limits, and no watermark. Upload a file up to 50 MB, wait while the conversion runs (a few seconds for most files), and download the result. Long clips take proportionally more time, but you always get a clean, complete output. Use it to convert a podcast episode to MP3 for your app, make a loop-friendly OGG for games, or shrink studio exports to shareable WAVs. Pair it with MP4 to MP3 for video audio extraction and QR Code Generator to drop a playable link anywhere. Audio Converter is straightforward, free, and private.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert between MP3, WAV, OGG, and M4A?

Yes. This tool encodes your audio into MP3, WAV, OGG, and M4A using high-quality encoders built into the browser engine.

Is the conversion done locally?

Yes, entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your audio never leaves your device.

Why might large audio files take longer?

Large or long files require more computation in the browser. We recommend a file up to 50 MB for the smoothest experience.

Will the audio quality be preserved?

We encode at a high bit rate by default, which preserves sound quality for music, podcasts, and voice recordings.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

The tool processes one file at a time to keep memory stable. Run it again for additional files.
