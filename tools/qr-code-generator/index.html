QR Code Generator

Create QR codes online for free — instantly and privately. This tool turns any URL, text, phone number, Wi-Fi network, or email address into a clean, scannable QR code that you can download as a high-resolution PNG. Perfect for restaurant menus, business cards, event posters, product packaging, and classroom materials. The code is generated locally in your browser, so the data you encode is never sent to any server — a genuine privacy win when you are encoding personal contact details or internal links. Output uses standard error-correction encoding that scans reliably in the camera apps on modern phones, and downloads crisp enough to print at any size. Speed matters: paste, generate, download — done in seconds, no account required and no watermark on your code. If you manage menus or signage, pair this tool with Image Converter to tweak the format or Image Resizer to get an exact print size. QR Code Generator is free, fast, and private — the simplest way to bridge the physical and digital worlds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free to generate unlimited QR codes?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up, no watermarks, and no expiry. Generate as many QR codes as you like.

Are my QR code payloads uploaded?

No. The QR code is generated locally in your browser. Your text, URL, or Wi-Fi data never leaves your device.

Can I scan the code on any phone?

Yes. The output PNG follows standard QR encoding, readable by the camera apps on iOS, Android, and desktop scanners.

What can I encode?

URLs, plain text, phone numbers, emails, Wi-Fi credentials, contact details, and any other short text payload.

What error-correction level do you use?

We use level M as a best balance between density and scannability, so codes tolerate minor damage or partial obstruction.
