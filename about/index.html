About ToolsAmerican

ToolsAmerican is a collection of simple, free, and genuinely private online tools. We began with a simple frustration: most "free" converters are anything but free — they upload your files to a server, stamp watermarks on your results, cap your file count, or silently sell your data. We built ToolsAmerican the way tools should be built: everything runs locally in your browser, and your files never leave your device.

Today you will find 12 tools across document, image, audio-video, and link categories — from merging PDFs and compressing images to extracting MP3 from video and generating QR codes. Each tool is a standalone page with clear instructions and a simple interface, and the entire library is free with no sign-up.

Our principles

🔒

Nothing is uploaded

Every tool uses WebAssembly and browser APIs to process your files directly on your device. When you merge a PDF or compress an image, the file never travels over the network. This makes the tools safe for contracts, photos, and other sensitive material.

⚡

Fast by design

Because there is no server round-trip, results appear as quickly as your device allows. No queues, no waiting for uploads, no "processing" spinner that lasts minutes.

🧼

Free, no watermarks

All tools are free to use without an account, without watermarks, and without artificial file limits. We keep the lights on with advertisements shown on some pages — and even those never touch your data.

🌱

Small and simple

We deliberately keep the site lightweight so pages load fast on slow connections and modest hardware. No heavy frameworks, no tracking clutter beyond what is strictly needed.

How we stay online

Building and hosting a site costs money. To keep every tool free, some pages show discreet advertisements. These ads never see your files, and you can read exactly how they work in our privacy policy.

Have a suggestion, a bug report, or an idea for a new tool? We would love to hear it — reach out on our contact page.
